== Introduction to the Hackathon

=== Overview

Welcome to the **Red Hat Developer Hub Enablement Day Hackathon**! Today, you'll step into the shoes of a Red Hat expert tasked with supporting one of our valued customers, Globex Corp. The objective is to enable Globex Corp to seamlessly integrate and utilise the powerful capabilities of the **Red Hat Developer Hub (RHDH)**.
